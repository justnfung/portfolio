WINNIPEG COMMUNITY COMPASS • SUMMER 2025

Winnipeg Community Compass

A self-taught, 7-day build that connected 5,000+ Manitobans to emergency resources, reached 1.3M+ views on national news, and sparked my passion for product management.

Role
Creator & Solo Full-Stack Developer
Timeline
Summer 2025 (1 Week Sprint)
Reach & Impact
5,000+ Evacuees • 1.3M+ YouTube Views
Tech Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Leaflet.js, Geolocation

The Challenge

Responding to a provincial emergency in under 7 days.

Manitoba's 2025 wildfire state of emergency displaced thousands of residents from their homes with minimal notice. Thousands of Winnipeggers, in true Friendly Manitoba™ spirit, piled in donations of pet food, clothes, and supplies to support wildfire evacuees.

The Problem

Critical information fragmentation when stakes were highest.

As one of these willing donors, I quickly spotted a problem: critical wildfire aid information was completely scattered across unorganized Facebook groups, Reddit threads, and community forums.

Scattered Data

Donation points and relief hubs were buried across dozens of Facebook posts with no map visualization.

High Friction

Existing platforms required logins or app downloads, inconvenient for users looking for places to donate in a pinch.

Zero Central Verification

Community-run businesses accepting supplies had no centralized channel to broadcast open hours or urgent needs.

Experiencing this frustration firsthand while trying to locate businesses accepting pet food donations, I realized that there was an opportunity to build a centralized, instant-access solution myself.

The Solution

Zero-barrier interactive mapping.

I built the Winnipeg Community Compass—a lightweight, mobile-optimized interactive map designed for maximum clarity and speed under crisis conditions.

Zero Sign-up Barriers

Eliminated all logins, downloads, and form friction so families in high-stress situations could access open aid instantly.

Client-Side Leaflet.js

Selected open-source Leaflet.js vector mapping to ensure map tiles rendered fast even on weak cellular networks during regional strain.

Real-Time Dynamic Filters

Categorized drop-off points, food banks, and shelters dynamically on the client side with zero page reload latency.
