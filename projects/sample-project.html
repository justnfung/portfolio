WINNIPEG COMMUNITY COMPASS • SUMMER 2025

Winnipeg Community Compass

A self-taught, 7-day build that connected 5,000+ evacuees to emergency resources, reached 1.3M+ views on national news, and sparked my passion for product management.

Role
Creator & Solo Full-Stack Developer
Timeline
Summer 2025 (1 Week Sprint)
Reach & Impact
5,000+ Evacuees • 1.3M+ YouTube Views
Tech Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Leaflet.js, Geolocation

The Challenge

Responding to a provincial emergency in under 7 days.

During the Summer 2025 wildfire emergency in Manitoba, thousands of residents were displaced from their homes with minimal notice. People across the province urgently wanted to donate supplies or find shelter, but critical emergency information was completely scattered across unorganized Facebook groups, Reddit threads, and community forums.

As a student with no formal web development background at the time, my challenge was to teach myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and mapping libraries on the fly—and ship a zero-barrier, production-ready web platform in less than seven days to serve families in crisis.

The Problem

Critical information fragmentation when stakes were highest.

When displaced families and willing donors opened their phones, they faced severe friction:

Scattered Data

Donation points and relief hubs were buried across dozens of Facebook posts with no map visualization.

High Friction

Existing crisis platforms required logins or app downloads—unusable for families operating on weak mobile networks.

Zero Central Verification

Community-run businesses accepting supplies had no centralized channel to broadcast open hours or urgent needs.

Experiencing this frustration firsthand while trying to locate local donation drop-offs, I realized that waiting for an official solution wasn't an option. I needed to build a centralized, instant-access solution myself.

The Solution

Winnipeg Community Compass: Zero-barrier interactive mapping.

I engineered Winnipeg Community Compass—a lightweight, mobile-optimized interactive map designed for maximum clarity and speed under crisis conditions.

Zero Sign-up Barriers

Eliminated all logins, downloads, and form friction so families in high-stress situations could access open aid instantly.

Client-Side Leaflet.js

Selected open-source Leaflet.js vector mapping to ensure map tiles rendered fast even on weak cellular networks during regional strain.

Real-Time Dynamic Filters

Categorized drop-off points, food banks, and shelters dynamically on the client side with zero page reload latency.

Live Demonstration: Interactive map scrolling, category filtering, and location popup cards in Winnipeg.

Surreal National News Coverage & 1.3M+ YouTube Views

CBC News featured Winnipeg Community Compass twice in national broadcasts. Seeing the YouTube broadcast segment hit over 1.3 Million views—and getting to record my parents' reaction while making our family dog a broadcast celebrity—was an unforgettable personal milestone.

Meeting Manitoba's Minister of Innovation

Following the CBC broadcast, Manitoba's Minister of Innovation and New Technology, Mike Moroz, reached out directly to thank me on behalf of the province. I met with the provincial government team to demonstrate the platform's architecture and share product insights to help inform the province's own internal crisis response tools.

Sparking My Product Management Career

Experiencing how rapid execution, technical problem-solving, and deep user empathy directly helped thousands of real people cemented my career goal: to lead and build products at the intersection of technology, business, and human impact.
